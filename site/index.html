Water law,
rule by rule.

Kazakhstan's Water Code and Ecological Code, split into 496 single rules: who must, may or must not do what, and for whom.

See the actors Browse the rules

Each article of the law is cut into single statements. Each statement names a body, what it must or may do, and who is on the receiving end. Put together, they show who actually runs water in Kazakhstan.

One rule, taken apart

Water Code, Article 25. The law lists what the subsoil authority does; this is one line of that list.

In English: the subsoil authority approves a groundwater abstraction project where abstraction exceeds one thousand cubic metres a day.

        Sources

        Method

        Statements are coded with the institutional grammar: attribute, deontic, aim, object, context. Ministry structures come from the supplementary tables of the paper.
